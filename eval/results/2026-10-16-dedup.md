# Concept dedup and shape: baseline of today's product

**2026-10-16** · `python3 eval/gepa/dedup/run.py evaluate --split test --repeats 3` · 5 test scenarios ×
3 repeats = 15 trials · generator Claude Code's default model · raw trials in
`2026-10-16-dedup-baseline.json`. Method: `eval/gepa/dedup/run.py` (header) and
`eval/fixtures/dedup/scenarios.json`. Baseline only: no change to the product has been measured yet.

## Cost

15 generation calls, no judges, 731,142 input tokens, 7,428 output, about $2.04 at list price, 213 s.
No call errors. (A 2-trial smoke run before it: 94,865 input tokens, $0.30.)

## What would make this wrong

- **5 scenarios, 15 trials.** Three repeats of one scenario are not three independent scenarios.
- **`dup_tokens` is a blunt instrument.** A created slug counts as a duplicate when it contains a token the
  scenario lists (`animation`, `idempoten`, `rmdir`). It cannot tell a true duplicate from a narrower
  sub-concept: `dispose-animation-controller` is flagged against `flutter-animation`, and under the
  entity design (one concept, a description that deepens) it belongs there, but a reader could disagree.
- **The model never saw the existing concepts.** That is the shipped behaviour (the tutor only learns
  matches from the response), so this is the right baseline, but it is not a fair test of the model.

## What would make this wrong

- **The test split is no longer untouched.** Matcher v2 (a concept counts as a candidate when everything it
  is called appears in the query) was written after reading v1's test results, where `idempotency-key`
  minted `idempotency-keys` beside `idempotency`. Train and val were not looked at before v2 and are the
  cleaner read, but they have no baseline: the product they would need to be compared with no longer exists
  here. Both are small (4 and 3 scenarios, 2 repeats).
- **`dup_tokens` still flags legitimate narrower concepts.** `commitlint`, `mutation-score-thresholds` and
  `ticker-provider-mixin` were created after the model was shown the candidates and chose `new: true`. The
  check counts them as duplicates. Whether they are is a judgement the eval does not make.
- **A name over three words fails the trial.** One test trial per run was lost to the tool rejecting a
  4-word name (score 0). A real session reads the error and resends, so this overstates the cost; the eval
  does not retry.
- **`react-effect-cleanup` scored 0.5 in both train trials** with nothing created and the expected concept
  not logged. The seeded catalogue may hold a better-fitting concept that the model chose; not checked.
- **5 scenarios, 15 trials.** Three repeats of one scenario are not three scenarios.

## Extraction did not regress

`extraction/run.py evaluate --split test --repeats 3`, 15 trials: recall (both judges) **0.95** against
0.91 at the 2026-10-11 baseline, precision 0.99 (0.99), mean score 0.767 (0.747 after the upsert fix),
10 of 15 trials passing every gate (9 of 15). Gate failures: `context_decision` 3, `valid_slugs` 1,
`context_file` 1. Those baselines are single runs on the same 5 fixtures, so a difference this size is
within the run-to-run spread the earlier note measured; the finding is that nothing got worse.
