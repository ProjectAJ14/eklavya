# Hook-text eval: baseline and one skip-wording candidate (issue #173)

Dated historical evidence. It says how the shipped checkpoint and Stop hook text
behaved on 12 frozen scenarios on this day, not how real sessions go.

## Setup

- **Harness:** `eval/hook-text-harness.mjs`, scenarios in `eval/fixtures/hook-text/scenarios.json` (12), scorer `mcp/src/eval/conversation-score.ts`.
- **Baseline:** the hook text at `1361366` (this branch after merging `main`; the wording is byte-for-byte what `main` ships, now built by `mcp/src/hooks/quiz-text-lib.ts`).
- **Candidate:** the same checkout with one sentence of the checkpoint text changed (below).
- **Model and settings:** the default `claude -p` model on the maintainer's subscription, identical for both sides. The model id was not pinned or recorded.
- **Trials:** 5 per scenario. One call per trial. No seeds. 0 unparsed or errored replies on every run.

## The run that does not count

The first 55-call baseline (11 scenarios) gave the model the hook text but not
the Eklavya tool definitions, which a real session always has. Without them it
invented `record_attempt` arguments (`correct_answer`, `concept_slug`,
`skipped: true`, no `grade`): 8 of its 11 failures. Two rules also misfired: the
gate rule read "before you can commit" as a promise that the gate was clear, and
the length rules counted the line announcing the resume. All three were fixed
before the runs below, and that run's numbers (44/55) are not used. It is the
issue's warning in practice: scenario defects first.

## The candidate, and why it does not ship

The checkpoint text says "If they pick Other or say skip, record it as grade 0,
teach the answer in two lines". Three other places say a skip is `declined`
with nothing taught: `skills/tutor/references/grading.md`, the plan's
`on_skip`, and `record_attempt`'s `outcome` field; so does the manual
(`first-session`, `grading-engine`). The candidate said instead: "If they say
skip, record grade 0 with outcome declined and carry on without teaching it. If
they pick Other and say they do not know, record grade 0 with outcome dont_know
and teach the answer in two lines. Either way, do not ask again."

It measured no better. On both sides, all 10 skip trials recorded `declined`,
grade 0, and taught nothing; the tool definitions outweigh the hook's sentence.
Each `checkpoint-skip` failure on either side is a reply like "Skipped. Back to
the test. I'm also adding a tampered-token case", with the resume line in the
same paragraph, which the 2-sentence `firstParagraph` rule counts. The
don't-know path taught on 5/5 on both sides. By the issue's rule (ship only what
wins) the wording stays as it is. The contradiction is real text, though, and
it would matter in a session that has not loaded the tool definitions; whether
to fix it for consistency alone is the maintainer's call.

## What this cannot show

The model is told the situation and every tool result in advance, the tutor
skill is not loaded, and five trials per scenario cannot separate 4/5 from 5/5.
Sentence limits are proxies for "one line of why". A clean trial shows the rule
is followed when read, not that real sessions improve; the delegation harness
remains the whole-session check.
